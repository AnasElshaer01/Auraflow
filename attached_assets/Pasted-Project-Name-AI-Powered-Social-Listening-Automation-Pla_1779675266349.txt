Project Name:
AI-Powered Social Listening & Automation Platform

Project Overview:
We are building a lean AI-powered social listening platform designed for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs). The platform helps businesses monitor online conversations about their brand, products, competitors, and industry trends in real time.

The goal is to provide an affordable and simple alternative to expensive enterprise social listening tools by focusing on automation, AI-generated insights, and fast setup.

The platform will collect mentions from social platforms, analyze sentiment using AI, and send smart alerts and summaries to business owners.

Target Customers:
- Small businesses
- E-commerce brands
- SaaS startups
- Agencies
- Local businesses
- Personal brands

Core Problems We Solve:
- Businesses miss customer complaints online
- No centralized monitoring for brand mentions
- Slow response to negative feedback
- No visibility into competitor conversations
- Manual social monitoring takes too much time

Lean MVP Goal:
The MVP should focus on validating demand as quickly as possible with the smallest useful feature set.

We are NOT building a full enterprise platform initially.

The MVP should only include:
1. Reddit mention monitoring
2. Keyword/brand tracking
3. AI sentiment analysis
4. Smart alerts
5. Daily AI summaries dashboard

Core MVP Features:

1. Social Monitoring
- Monitor Reddit posts and comments
- Track:
  - Brand names
  - Keywords
  - Competitor names
- Store mentions in the database

2. AI Sentiment Analysis
Use AI to classify mentions into:
- Positive
- Neutral
- Negative

Optional:
- Urgency detection
- Complaint detection

3. Smart Alerts
Send alerts when:
- Negative mentions appear
- High engagement mentions detected
- Competitor mentions spike

Alert channels:
- Slack
- Email

4. AI Daily Summaries
Generate daily AI summaries containing:
- Top mentions
- Main complaints
- Trending discussions
- Overall sentiment overview

5. Dashboard
Simple dashboard with:
- Mentions feed
- Sentiment statistics
- Recent alerts
- AI summaries

Important Development Notes:
- Build fast and lean
- Avoid overengineering
- Use managed services whenever possible
- Reuse existing tools instead of building infrastructure
- Focus on validating customer demand quickly
- Prioritize usability and automation over complex analytics

What We Are NOT Building Initially:
- Multi-platform social integrations
- Complex ML pipelines
- Custom workflow builders
- Enterprise analytics
- White-label support
- Advanced team permissions
- Custom AI model training

Primary MVP Objective:
Launch quickly with a functional product that can:
- Monitor Reddit mentions
- Analyze sentiment
- Send alerts
- Generate AI summaries

The objective is to get early users and validate the market before expanding features.
